NightScript Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version: NightScript does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Your voice recordings stay on your device. Period.

Voice recordings

NightScript records your voice only when you tap the record button, and only to let you play your own affirmations back to yourself. Recordings are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded, never sent to us, and never shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone access

The app asks for microphone permission solely to make your recordings. The microphone is active only while you are recording. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

Data we collect

None. NightScript has no user accounts, no analytics trackers, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party data collection in the app. We do not collect your name, email, location, usage data, or any other personal information.

Purchases

If a paid upgrade is offered, payment is handled entirely by the platform's store (for example, Apple's App Store). We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

NightScript is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the current version will always be at nightscript.app/privacy.html with an updated date at the top.
