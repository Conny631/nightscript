How NightScript Works

1. Record. Choose mantras and record them in your own voice.

2. Descend. At bedtime, put on headphones. Binaural beats gently guide your brain from alert (alpha) down through theta — the hypnagogic doorway state where the subconscious is most open to suggestion.

3. Program. Your own voice repeats your chosen mantras softly as you drift off, planting them in the most receptive minutes of your day.

4. Reinforce. Use the Morning Primer (alpha 10 Hz) for 10 minutes on waking, keep your streak alive, and re-record mantras every 21 nights as your goals evolve.

The 21-Night Program

Pick 3–5 mantras maximum. Repetition beats variety — the subconscious learns by hearing the same message night after night. After 21 nights, rotate in a new set.

Safety

NightScript is a relaxation and self-development tool, not medical treatment. Do not use binaural beats if you have epilepsy, a seizure disorder, or a heart-rhythm device without asking your doctor. Never listen while driving. Keep volume low — this is a whisper to your mind, not a concert.

Privacy

Without an account, everything stays on this device only. When you create a free account, your recordings and settings are backed up privately to your account so you can restore them on any phone or computer. Your recordings are never shared, never analyzed, never used for anything but playing them back to you. No tracking, no ads.
